# MiSTer-VCMI v0.2.0

Adds two mods and three hardware-validated performance fixes for hero-movement
stutter, on top of the [v0.1.0](0.1.0.md) preview. It still runs
[VCMI](https://github.com/vcmi/vcmi) 1.7.5, the open-source Heroes of Might and
Magic III engine, on the ARM side of a MiSTer (DE10-Nano). The game is an
ordinary Linux program; nothing is installed outside `/media/fat`. You need your
own copy of the Heroes III data: it is not included.

## Install

1. Unzip `MiSTer-VCMI-v0.2.0.zip` onto the root of the MiSTer's SD card, merging
   with what is there. This creates `/media/fat/vcmi` and one
   `/media/fat/Scripts/vcmi*.sh` per launcher (`vcmi`, `vcmi-hota`, `vcmi-wog`).
2. Copy the `Data`, `Maps` and `Mp3` folders from your Heroes III install (GOG
   "Complete" was tested) into `/media/fat/vcmi/data`. `Data/VIDEO.VID` is not
   needed.
3. On the MiSTer press F12, choose **Scripts**, and run **vcmi** for the base
   game. `vcmi-hota` and `vcmi-wog` play those mods, but fall back to the base
   game until you fetch the mod's own files yourself on a PC with the project's
   `tools/fetch-hota.sh` or `tools/fetch-wog.sh` and copy them to
   `/media/fat/vcmi/data/Mods` (see the
   [README](https://github.com/aquasock/MiSTer-VCMI#mods)); mod files are not
   part of this zip.

The screen blinks as the HDMI output switches to 800x600, which your display
scales to fill the screen. Quit from the game's own menu and the launcher
switches your display back to 1080p60. Full instructions are in
`vcmi/INSTALL.txt` and the
[README](https://github.com/aquasock/MiSTer-VCMI#readme).

You need a MiSTer with a DE10-Nano, a USB mouse and keyboard, and an HDMI
display that accepts 800x600 at 60 Hz.

## What's new since v0.1.0

- **Horn of the Abyss (HotA)** and **In The Wake of Gods (WoG)**, two fan
  expansions, playable from their own Scripts entries.
- **Hero movement is measurably smoother.** SDL's ARM NEON blitters are now
  built in, VCMI's adventure-map renderer merges adjacent tile redraws into
  fewer, wider ones, and a plain camera pan now reuses the previous frame
  (shifted) instead of recompositing the whole screen every frame. Measured on
  hardware: sustained walking away from a turn now holds a tight 37-57 ms
  worst-case frame gap, down from 100-900 ms before. The largest stutter spikes
  during movement, which come from a different, unaddressed cause (VCMI's own
  per-tile content refresh, driven by an animation timer rather than camera
  movement), are unchanged; movement right after ending a turn is also
  unaffected, since that roughness comes from turn processing, not panning.
- **Quitting no longer shows a black screen** where the OSD's "press ENTER to
  continue" prompt should be; the console is now reliably restored to text mode
  regardless of how the game exited.

## What works

- Menus, single-scenario games, saving and loading, computer players' turns,
  battles, and now two mods (HotA and WoG).
- Mouse and keyboard, with USB hotplug (including unplug/replug of the same or
  a different device, tested on hardware this cycle).
- Music and sound effects through the MiSTer's audio path (HDMI and analog).
- Nothing needs `MiSTer.ini` changes and nothing outside `/media/fat` is
  touched.

## Known limitations

- Hero movement is smoother but not perfectly smooth: occasional 100-900 ms
  stutter spikes remain, and movement right after ending a turn is still rough.
  See "What's new" above.
- 800x600 is the supported size. 1024x600 widescreen works
  (`MISTER_OUTPUT_MODE=1024x600` in `vcmi/env.sh`) but costs about 44% more CPU
  per frame.
- No video playback (intros and campaign videos are skipped).
- Only the GOG Heroes III Complete data has been tested. Other editions,
  campaigns in depth and networked multiplayer are untested. HotA and WoG are
  the only mods tested; loading both at once, or a mod not covered by
  `tools/fetch-*.sh`, is untested.
- Your original HDMI mode is not saved; the launcher restores 1080p60. Set
  `MISTER_RESTORE_MODE` if yours differs.
- VCMI's launcher, map editor and lobby server are not included.

# MiSTer-VCMI v0.1.0

First public preview. It runs [VCMI](https://github.com/vcmi/vcmi) 1.7.5, the open-source Heroes of Might and Magic III
engine, on the ARM side of a MiSTer (DE10-Nano). The game is an ordinary Linux program; nothing is installed outside
`/media/fat`. You need your own copy of the Heroes III data: it is not included.

## Install

1. Unzip `MiSTer-VCMI-v0.1.0.zip` onto the root of the MiSTer's SD card, merging with what is there. This creates
   `/media/fat/vcmi` and `/media/fat/Scripts/vcmi.sh`.
2. Copy the `Data`, `Maps` and `Mp3` folders from your Heroes III install (GOG "Complete" was tested) into
   `/media/fat/vcmi/data`. `Data/VIDEO.VID` is not needed.
3. On the MiSTer press F12, choose **Scripts**, and run **vcmi**.

The screen blinks as the HDMI output switches to 800x600, which your display scales to fill the screen. Quit from the
game's own menu and the launcher switches your display back to 1080p60. Full instructions are in `vcmi/INSTALL.txt`
and the [README](https://github.com/aquasock/MiSTer-VCMI#readme).

You need a MiSTer with a DE10-Nano, a USB mouse and keyboard, and an HDMI display that accepts 800x600 at 60 Hz.

## What works

- Menus, single-scenario games, saving and loading, computer players' turns, battles.
- Mouse and keyboard, with USB hotplug.
- Music and sound effects through the MiSTer's audio path (HDMI and analog), gapless in testing.
- Nothing needs `MiSTer.ini` changes and nothing outside `/media/fat` is touched.

Measured at 800x600: the adventure map runs at about 55-59 fps at rest and about 43-52 fps while heroes move or the
map pans; menus run at about 30 fps; computer players take about 4-8 seconds per turn early in a game.

## Known limitations

- Hero movement and panning are not perfectly smooth (both Cortex-A9 cores are busy).
- 800x600 is the supported size. 1024x600 widescreen works (`MISTER_OUTPUT_MODE=1024x600` in `vcmi/env.sh`) but costs
  about 44% more CPU per frame.
- No video playback (intros and campaign videos are skipped).
- Only the GOG Heroes III Complete data has been tested. Other editions, mods and networked multiplayer are untested.
- Your original HDMI mode is not saved; the launcher restores 1080p60. Set `MISTER_RESTORE_MODE` if yours differs.
- VCMI's launcher, map editor and lobby server are not included.
